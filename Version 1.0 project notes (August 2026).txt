Version 1.0 (August 2026)

Features:
- Downloaded historical prices
- Constructed thematic portfolios
- Calculated cumulative returns
- Annualized return
- Annualized volatility
- Sharpe ratio
- Maximum drawdown
- Fixed-composition analysis
- Correlation matrix
- Growth of $1 chart
- Rolling volatility chart
- Risk vs. Return scatter plot

Known limitations:
- Daily equal-weight methodology
- No transaction costs
- No monthly rebalancing
- No factor attribution
